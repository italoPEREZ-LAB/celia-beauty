# Imágenes de Celia Beauty

Generadas con la herramienta integrada Imagegen como composiciones ilustrativas. La dirección visual procede de las cinco referencias proporcionadas: lavanda, rosa, violeta, productos metálicos y plata. No representan inventario real.

Los WebP están en public/assets. Los PNG originales se conservan en work/celia-beauty dentro del espacio de trabajo.

## Prompts utilizados

### hero.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: imagery for CELIA BEAUTY beauty store and salon website. Wide landscape 3:2 premium beauty campaign still life. A lush sculptural arrangement of unbranded haircare pump bottles, serum bottles, cosmetic jars and a few salon combs and rounded brush, polished metallic silver, dusty rose pink, dark plum and lavender packaging. Products float subtly above reflective lilac podiums, elegant lavender sprigs at the edges, graceful chrome ribbon arcs, tasteful glossy spheres. Arrange the cluster predominantly in the right two thirds, very light lavender gradient left. Soft cinematic studio light, luxurious playful modern beauty store aesthetic. No people. Palette: lavender #b9a0df, pale lilac #eee9f6, muted rose #be8aac, deep purple #372648, silver chrome. Photorealistic polished 3D product advertising aesthetic. No text, lettering, brand logos, prices, website interfaces, watermarks, collage or borders. Illustrative products, not actual branded inventory.
